Scientific Positioning & Ethical Scope

System positioning, non-diagnostic clinical boundaries, and research governance standards.

Algorithmic Decision Support, Not Autonomous Diagnosis

CognitiveLens AI is engineered to identify persistent longitudinal deviations in spoken language relative to an individual's own calibrated baseline. It does not replace neuropsychological batteries or neurological clinical evaluation.

1. Intra-Individual Calibration

Standard cognitive screens compare subjects against demographic averages, missing high-reserve decline. CognitiveLens AI compares every observation to the patient's own baseline.

2. Persistence Requirement

An isolated bad day due to acute fatigue or stress does not indicate neurodegeneration. Multiple orthogonal markers must persist across consecutive longitudinal sessions.

3. Explainable Linguistic Features

Signals correlate directly with measurable speech metrics (TTR, clause depth, pause duration) rather than opaque black-box embeddings.

4. Evidence Grounding (No Hallucinations)

Caregiver advice is bound strictly to peer-reviewed literature via the Evidence Battle framework, pairing clinical evidence with contextual qualifiers.
